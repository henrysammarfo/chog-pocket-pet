# Chog Snack Hunt

Gameplay rebranded from [MattSurabian/DuckHunt-JS](https://github.com/MattSurabian/DuckHunt-JS) by Matt Surabian.

Licence: MIT. The full text is in `LICENSE`.

Every Nintendo sprite from the upstream game is rebuilt. Targets are chips, a donut, and a MON coin. The reveal is a Chog. No NES dog, duck, or background is shipped.
