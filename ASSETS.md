## Arcade

The six games use original pictures in `public/arcade/`: a peach Chog (flap, jump, duck, and reveal), neon pipes, a rug, a red candle, chips, a donut, a MON coin, clouds, and a skyline. They were drawn for this pocket and keyed so the background is transparent. They are not the upstream game art, not Nintendo art, and not the Chrome dinosaur. Floppy Chog, Chog Dash, and Chog Snack Hunt replace those pictures entirely. Chog Tower 3D is lit three.js slabs with shadows. Gameplay licences and credits are in `licenses/arcade/`. The Kenney models listed above are not used inside the games.

## Motion Sites prompts

The layout of three screens follows free prompts on [motionsites.ai](https://motionsites.ai/):

| Prompt id | Title | What we kept | What we changed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `3d-collectible-hero` | 3D Collectible Hero | Full-viewport carousel, four roles, 650ms ease, grain, Anton ghost word, arrow buttons | Brand and copy are Chog. Figurines are the original pictures above, not the prompt's sample files. |
| `cozypaws` | CozyPaws | Mint page, DM Serif headline, word-pop, header, side cards, three bottom photos | Shop words and photos are the pocket, the jar, and the Chogs. |
| `orbis-nft-landing` | Orbis NFT | Navy field, Anton, Condiment, neon green, liquid glass, four sections, mood scores | Words are the Chog pocket. The prompt's video files are not listed as CC0, so those slots use the original stills with a slow zoom. |

Other prompts on the site (Autumn Fox, Meadowbell, 3D Character Studio, Pet Diagnostics) were read and left out. Autumn Fox is a fox with a drink, Meadowbell is a hillside cartoon, and the others are not a pocket pet. The 3D pet in the game uses three.js in the same spirit as the Autumn Fox prompt's canvas: a model on a full stage, fading in when it is ready.
